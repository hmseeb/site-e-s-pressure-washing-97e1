FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas does E&S Pressure Washing serve?

We’re based in Callahan, FL and serve all of Nassau County, plus Jacksonville, St. Augustine, Starke and Kingsland, GA. We’re also accountable across Duval, St. Johns and Bradford counties. Local Callahan and Nassau County customers get scheduling priority.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. E&S Pressure Washing LLC is fully licensed and insured, and we are a BBB Accredited Business. That coverage extends to commercial property work as well.

What is the E&S Clean Promise?

It’s our guarantee: if you’re not completely satisfied with the results, let us know within 48 hours and we’ll come back and make it right — no excuses, no hassle. It’s a big part of why we hold a perfect 5.0 Google rating.

Do you offer free estimates?

Absolutely. Estimates are always free and there’s never pressure to book. Call (904) 822-8665 or send the quote form on this page and Edward will get back to you with honest, upfront pricing.

What factors affect the price of a job?

Square footage, the surface type, how much build-up there is, access and setup, and how many services you bundle together. Because Edward quotes every job himself, you get a real number for your property — not a generic online calculator.

Do you offer discounts or bundle pricing for multiple services?

Yes. Combining services — like house washing with a driveway and walkway clean on the same visit — is the most cost-effective way to book, since we’re already set up on site. Ask about bundling when you call.

Do you work weekends?

We work Monday through Saturday, 7AM to 7PM. We’re closed Sundays. Saturday slots fill up fastest, so book ahead if that’s what you need.

What happens if it rains on my scheduled day?

Florida weather happens. If conditions won’t let us do the job right, we’ll contact you and reschedule to the next available slot at no penalty and no rescheduling fee.

Is soft washing safe for my siding and roof?

Yes — that’s exactly why we use it. Soft washing uses low pressure and eco-safe detergents to kill and remove algae, mold and mildew at the source, instead of blasting delicate surfaces with high pressure. It’s the safe choice for vinyl, stucco, painted siding and shingles.

How often should I have my driveway cleaned?

For most Northeast Florida homes, once a year keeps organic growth and stains from setting in. Shaded driveways, heavy tree cover or high humidity can push that toward every 6–12 months.

Will pressure washing damage my concrete or pavers?

Not when it’s done correctly. We match the pressure to the surface and use a professional surface cleaner for even results — no wand marks, no etching, and no blowing the joint sand out of your pavers.

Can pressure washing remove mold, mildew and algae?

Yes. Our eco-safe detergents treat the organic growth at the root rather than just rinsing the surface, so your home stays cleaner noticeably longer than a plain water rinse.

Is fence cleaning safe before staining or sealing?

It’s recommended. Cleaning strips away mildew, gray oxidation and dirt so the stain or sealer actually penetrates the wood and lasts. Let the fence dry fully after cleaning before you apply any finish.

Do you offer commercial service contracts?

We do. We handle commercial house washing, driveway cleaning, concrete cleaning and fence cleaning for storefronts, offices, rentals and HOA properties on recurring schedules. Call for a commercial bid.

Can you work after business hours for commercial properties?

Yes. We can schedule around your operating hours so cleaning never blocks your entrance, your parking or your customers.
